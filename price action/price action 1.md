#标题

- tick: smallest move on stock or futures chart
- pip: smallest move on a Forex chart
- point: group of ticks or pips
- lot: size of a trade
- handle: round number of price

When placing trade, pay attention to **context**: big picture, all of the bars to the left of current bar.

Trade based on price action. Volume is a false god, that beginners hope will protect them from a painful loss.
